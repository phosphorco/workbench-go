This is the remaining instrument brief, not passing evidence or an alternative
activation contract. Product outcomes come from `docs/context-declarations.md`.
Use the real installed binary layout with private Pkl and runtime-lock metadata;
an ambient Pkl lookup must not make a missing private capability pass.
